2a:T559,The green window is computed from the trailing 30 days of hourly values (price and renewable share of generation), each hour independent. A green hour has price at or below the 30th percentile of the trailing price distribution AND renewable share at or above the 70th percentile of the trailing renewable-share distribution. A red hour is the exact inverse through the same code path: price at or above the 70th percentile AND renewable share at or below the 30th percentile. The rendered window is the hour interval (at least two contiguous wall-clock hours) that recurs most often as a whole: the number of trailing days on which every hour of the interval qualifies under the rule above. Ties prefer the longer interval, then the earliest start. Its frequency share is the share of trailing days on which the full interval qualifies. Hours qualifying outside the window still count in the frequency strip and the risk indicators individually. Percentiles use linear interpolation on the sorted hourly sample. Basis: renewable share of generation (NL generation mix); price is the NL day-ahead price.
